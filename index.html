Charlie Oh | Playwright & Screenwriter

Charlie Oh is a writer living in Brooklyn.

His plays include “LONG” (Kennedy Center’s Paula Vogel Award, Mark Twain Prize, Relentless Award Honors), “COLEMAN ’72” (world premiere South Coast Rep, published 2025 with Restless Books, Paul Stephen Lim Playwriting Award, Los Angeles Drama Critics Circle Award nominee), “WHITE MONKEY” (Goodman New Stages, Future Labs), “THE DISRUPTORS” (The Juilliard School, Sloan Commission), and “IT’S LIKE AN AMAZING TIME TO BE ALIVE RIGHT NOW (!), OR, THE CRYPTO PLAY.”

His work has been developed at Manhattan Theatre Club, South Coast Rep, The Lark, Second Stage, The American Musical Theatre Project, The Goodman, Peacedale Global Arts, and is a past member, fellow, or alumni of Ensemble Studio Theater’s Youngblood, Ars Nova Play Group, Page 73’s Writers Group, The Lark, and the Catwalk Writer’s Residency, among others.

He’s a Core Writer at the Playwrights’ Center, an Artistic Research Fellow at the Folger Shakespeare Library, and is the inaugural recipient of the Steppenwolf + Stephen Sondheim Foundation commission.

He also holds commissions from Manhattan Theater Club + the Alfred P. Sloan Foundation, South Coast Rep, and has developed projects for television with Amazon Studios and Universal Studios.

Education: The Juilliard School’s Lila Acheson Wallace American Playwrights Program, Northwestern University.
